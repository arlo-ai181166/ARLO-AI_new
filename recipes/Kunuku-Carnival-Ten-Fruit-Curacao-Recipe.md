# Kunuku Carnival
**Ten-Fruit Curaçao Crown for 10**

A tall layered glass bowl of coconut cream, soursop mousse, mango–passion gel, papaya–patia, kenepa, guava, and dark tamarind caramel.

**Total time:** about 2 hours active + 4 hours chill  
**Serves:** 10 generous portions

The 10 Curaçao fruits used:
1. Kenepa (Spanish lime / guinep)
2. Tamarind
3. Mango
4. Papaya
5. Soursop (sorsaka)
6. Lime
7. Patia (local melon)
8. Coconut
9. Guava
10. Passion fruit (maracuja)

---

## Ingredients

### Coconut–lime cream
- 800 ml full-fat coconut milk
- 200 ml coconut cream
- 120 g sugar
- Zest of 4 Curaçao limes + juice of 3
- 8 g powdered gelatin (or 4 sheets), bloomed
- Pinch of salt

### Soursop mousse
- 600 g ripe soursop pulp, seeds removed (about 2 large fruits)
- 80 g sugar
- Juice of 1 lime
- 300 ml cold heavy cream, whipped to soft peaks
- 6 g gelatin, bloomed

### Mango–passion gel
- 500 g ripe mango flesh
- Pulp of 8 passion fruits
- 60 g sugar
- Juice of 1 lime
- 6 g gelatin, bloomed

### Tamarind caramel
- 180 g tamarind pulp (from pods, seeds strained)
- 150 g sugar
- 80 ml water
- 40 g butter
- Pinch of salt

### Fresh fruit layer
- 2 ripe papayas, peeled, seeded, cut in 2 cm cubes
- 1 large patia (or ½ large local melon), cubed the same size
- 400 g kenepa, peeled
- 3 ripe guavas, sliced thin (or 200 g guava paste cut into small diamonds)
- 2 extra limes, supremed (segments only)

### Crunch & finish
- 200 g toasted coconut flakes (half left large and golden)
- 80 g crushed toasted coconut cookies or shortbread
- Extra lime zest
- A few passion-fruit seeds
- Optional: edible flowers or mint

---

## Method

### 1. Coconut–lime cream
Warm coconut milk, coconut cream, sugar, lime zest, and salt until the sugar dissolves. Do not boil. Off the heat, stir in bloomed gelatin and lime juice. Cool until just starting to thicken. Pour a 2–3 cm layer into the bottom of a very large glass trifle bowl (or divide between two bowls). Chill 45 minutes until set enough to take the next layer.

### 2. Soursop mousse
Blend soursop with sugar and lime juice until silky. Warm a small splash of the puree, dissolve the gelatin in it, then fold back into the rest. Cool until cool but still fluid. Fold in the whipped cream. Spoon over the coconut layer. Chill 1 hour.

### 3. Mango–passion gel
Blend mango, passion pulp, sugar, and lime. Dissolve gelatin in a little warmed puree, mix back, cool until syrupy. Pour over the soursop. Chill 1 hour until firm.

### 4. Tamarind caramel
Cook sugar and water to a deep amber. Carefully add tamarind pulp (it will seize — keep stirring), then butter and salt. Simmer 2–3 minutes until glossy and pourable. Cool to warm.

### 5. Assemble the crown
On the set mango–passion layer, pile the papaya and patia cubes in a loose ring. Tuck in guava slices and lime segments. Scatter most of the kenepa. Spoon a little tamarind caramel in thin ribbons so it pools in the fruit. Sprinkle crushed cookies and half the toasted coconut.

Just before serving, add the remaining toasted coconut in big shards on top, more lime zest, passion seeds, and a last drizzle of tamarind caramel.
